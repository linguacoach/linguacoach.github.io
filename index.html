A few quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Are lessons online?

Yes. Lingua Coach is designed for online lessons, so you can learn from home or wherever is convenient for you.

How much do lessons cost?

A single lesson costs €25. A pack of 10 lessons costs €230, which works out to €23 per lesson and saves €20 compared with buying ten single lessons.

Which languages can I study?

You can request English, German or French lessons. Share your level and goals in the booking form so the lesson can be tailored to you.

Can I pay online?

Once you've submitted your details, you can pay online either with a credit card or via Paypal.
